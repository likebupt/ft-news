## Microsoft Research
- **Echoverse** introduces deep, evolving environments for training computer-use agents on realistic multi-step workflows, rather than just adding more static tasks. For post-training, it suggests agent finetuning will benefit from environment-driven curricula and co-evolving evals that better capture real desktop/workflow behavior. [Read more](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/echoverse-deep-evolving-environments-for-computer-use-agents/)
- **EvoLib** proposes turning deployment experience into evolving knowledge, extracting reusable skills and insights instead of only storing more context or memories. This matters for finetuning because it points to a practical continual post-training loop: distill trajectories/feedback into transferable capabilities that improve future task performance. [Read more](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/evolib-turning-experience-into-evolving-knowledge/)

## Amazon Science
- **ControlG** adapts ideas from industrial controllers to multitask learning, dynamically allocating capacity across objectives instead of averaging conflicting parameter updates. For finetuning, this is relevant to multi-objective post-training (e.g. helpfulness, safety, style, domain adaptation) where gradient interference often hurts final model quality. [Read more](https://www.amazon.science/blog/how-controllers-from-industrial-machinery-can-coordinate-multitask-machine-learning)
- **PatientAgentBench** is a new benchmark that simulates patient records, clinical vignettes, and patient conversations to evaluate patient-facing health agents more realistically. It matters for finetuning because domain post-training in healthcare needs interaction-level evals for safety, dialogue quality, and factuality—not just static medical QA scores. [Read more](https://www.amazon.science/blog/a-new-benchmark-for-evaluating-patient-facing-health-ai-agents)

## Together AI
- **ThunderAgent** is a program-aware scheduler that speeds agentic inference for synthetic data generation by more than 2x and scales efficiently across nodes. For finetuning, faster agent rollouts reduce the cost of generating synthetic trajectories, tool-use traces, and other post-training data at large scale. [Read more](https://www.together.ai/blog/thunderagent)

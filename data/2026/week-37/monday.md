### Research / Academia

- **GLOW: Graph-Language Co-Encoding for Agentic Workflow Performance Prediction** *(Most relevant)* — Uses graph + language co-encoding to predict agentic workflow performance, aiming to reduce the cost of workflow optimization. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15751)
- **Towards Understanding Pause Token Fine-Tuning Dynamics: A Mode Retention Perspective** — Argues pause-token fine-tuning may help reasoning less by adding compute-like expressivity and more by preserving useful pretrained behaviors during post-training. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04489)
- **ConsensusBench: Benchmark of Consensus Nodes for LLM Reasoning via Outcome Reward Densifying** — Introduces a benchmark for analyzing RL-based reasoning gains, especially for GRPO-style methods and denser outcome rewards. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04648)
- **Generating Constructive Feedback on Stories via Reinforcement Learning** — Applies RL to improve LLM-generated constructive feedback for creative writing, targeting expensive human-review workflows. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04824)
- **On Epistemic Diversity in Large Language Models** — Proposes evaluating LLMs not just on accuracy, but on the diversity of perspectives and explanations they can generate. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04835)
- **Leveraging Low-Level Symbolic Competences for Unsupervised Grounding in Hallucination Detection** — Presents an unsupervised hallucination-detection approach that uses symbolic competencies for grounding without heavy labeled-data requirements. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05025)
- **Compression Beyond the Uncompressed: A Two-Stage Training Recipe for Soft Context Compression in RAG** — Introduces a two-stage training recipe for soft context compression in RAG. *(Link/details were truncated in the source provided.)*

### Key Takeaways

- **Post-training remains the center of gravity**: multiple papers focus on RL, reward shaping, and lightweight reasoning-tuning methods.
- **Evaluation is broadening**: researchers are looking beyond accuracy toward workflow performance prediction, reward-dense reasoning benchmarks, and epistemic diversity.
- **Reliability and efficiency are converging themes**: hallucination detection, context compression in RAG, and lower-cost workflow optimization all point to more deployable LLM systems.

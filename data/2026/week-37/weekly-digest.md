## TL;DR
This week’s clearest signal is that the fine-tuning market is shifting from raw model support to workflow quality: Together AI raised the bar with better observability, dataset validation, PEFT controls, and lower pricing, while also introducing a sharply cheaper preemptible compute tier. That matters because enterprise buyers increasingly evaluate tuning platforms on failed-run reduction, iteration speed, and effective cost per successful model update—not just whether tuning is technically possible. At the same time, Hugging Face showed a more portable path to RL-style post-training with async GRPO + LoRA on loosely coupled infrastructure, which could make reinforcement tuning cheaper and less dependent on premium clusters. Finally, Databricks’ evaluation-first customer story reinforces that the next control point in this market is not training jobs alone, but the closed loop between evals, data collection, and retraining.

## Top Stories
- Together AI expanded its fine-tuning service with more open-weight models, live experiment metrics, Expert LoRA, early stopping, tokenized dataset previews, pre-flight validation, and lower prices on some models. This matters because it reframes platform competition around end-to-end tuning ergonomics and cost efficiency—areas where enterprise PMs notice immediate ROI and lower operational risk. [Read more](https://www.together.ai/blog/together-ai-expands-fine-tuning-service-with-more-models-live-metrics-and-finer-controls)
- Together AI launched preemptible GPU compute at 50% of on-demand pricing with a five-minute drain window before interruption. This is strategically important because it turns checkpointing and resume reliability into a product differentiator and puts direct pricing pressure on managed post-training workloads. [Read more](https://www.together.ai/blog/introducing-preemptible-compute-the-same-compute-half-the-price)
- Hugging Face described running async GRPO with LoRA on HF Jobs using object storage and a proxy layer instead of tightly coupled NCCL-style communication. If this approach proves durable, RL-style post-training becomes easier to run on cheaper, more flexible infrastructure, which could weaken premium positioning based solely on specialized cluster topology. [Read more](https://huggingface.co/blog/asyncgrpo-lora-hfjobs)
- Databricks highlighted Zepto’s evaluation-first approach to scaling customer-support agents with Databricks and MLflow. The broader implication is that customers are increasingly deciding when and how to finetune through evaluation pipelines, so the platform that owns eval-driven retraining loops can capture more of the post-training stack. [Read more](https://www.databricks.com/blog/evaluation-first-ai-agents-how-zepto-scales-customer-support-databricks-and-mlflow)

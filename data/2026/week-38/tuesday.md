## Research / Academia

- **[RLCSD: Reinforcement Learning with Contrastive On-Policy Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11709)** — Uses contrastive on-policy self-distillation to provide denser token-level supervision during RL for reasoning models, improving credit assignment beyond sparse verifiable rewards.
- **[Continual Learning for Sequential Personalization of Small Language Models: A Stability Monitoring Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27634)** — Studies continual personalization of small on-device LMs, with a focus on monitoring stability and forgetting over time.
- **[The Intruder Threshold: A Spectral Law for LoRA Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23711)** — Theorizes when LoRA creates “intruder dimensions” that may drive catastrophic forgetting, with implications for rank/scaling choices.
- **[Non-Asymptotic Global Convergence of PPO-Clip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16565)** — Provides global convergence guarantees for PPO-Clip, helping close the gap between RL theory and common LLM post-training practice.
- **Internalize the Temperature: On-Policy Self-Distillation as Policy Reheater for Reinforcement Learning** — Explores on-policy self-distillation as a policy “reheater” during RL. *(Link not provided in source.)*
- **[REDDIT: Correcting Model-Generated Timestamp Drift in ASR without Forgetting via Replay-Based Distribution Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05364)** — Repairs timestamp drift in autoregressive ASR via replay-based distribution editing, without relying on aligners or causing catastrophic forgetting.
- **[ReCAST: Reward Credit Assignment across Timesteps for Online Diffusion Reinforcement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13425)** — Introduces timestep-level credit assignment for online diffusion RL

## Research / Academia (arXiv)

- [**Extending FunctionGemma for Practical On-Device Mobile Function Calling**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25373) — Pushes FunctionGemma toward **on-device mobile assistants**, mapping natural-language requests to **local system actions** instead of primarily web APIs; emphasizes **privacy-preserving, low-latency** deployment.
- [**From Experts to Sub-experts: Fine-grained Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning for MoE LLMs**](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=From+Experts+to+Sub-experts%3A+Fine-grained+Parameter-Efficient+Fine-Tuning+for+MoE+LLMs&searchtype=all&source=header) — Explores **finer-grained PEFT for MoE models**, shifting adaptation from whole experts to smaller **sub-expert** units to improve specialization efficiency.
- [**Trains but Doesn't Learn: A Post-Training Delivery Benchmark for LLM Agents as Forward-Deployed Engineers**](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=Trains+but+Doesn%27t+Learn%3A+A+Post-Training+Delivery+Benchmark+for+LLM+Agents+as+Forward-Deployed+Engineers&searchtype=all&source=header) — Introduces a **post-training delivery benchmark** for **PTaaS** workflows, evaluating LLM agents more like **forward-deployed engineers** than static benchmark-takers.

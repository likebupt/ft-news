### Academic / Open Research (arXiv)

- **Regime Boundary Alignment for Evidence-Gated Question Answering:** Examines why RAG models often answer even when retrieved evidence is missing, tracing it to a mismatch between training stages; proposes aligning the “answer vs. abstain/defer” boundary so models rely more faithfully on evidence. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37491)

- **Evaluating Bounded Autonomy in Regulated Agentic AI:** Introduces **RegLLM**, a diagnostic harness for regulated agent workflows that tracks trust signals such as citation validity, grounding, schema compliance, escalation behavior, and runtime governance, aimed at testing bounded autonomy rather than unconstrained agent performance. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37501)

- **Correct, Don’t Delete: Mitigating Emergent Misalignment with Corrective Supervision:** Targets emergent misalignment caused by fine-tuning on narrow harmful datasets, arguing that corrective supervision can preserve general alignment better than simply deleting or suppressing problematic behaviors. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37624)

- **Can Vision-Language Models Stay Helpful When Facing Implicit Risks?:** Focuses on cross-modal safety failures where image and text are benign separately but unsafe jointly; proposes **Intent-Privilege OPSD** to improve safety-helpfulness tradeoffs for VLM post-training. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37837)

- **It’s All Training: A Fully Synthetic Single-Stage Recipe for LLMs:** Proposes replacing messy web-crawl-centric pipelines with a fully synthetic, single-stage training recipe designed to better support reasoning and instruction-following without heavy mid-/post-training complexity. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37891)

- **The Unequal Influence of Bad Advice:** Uses training data attribution to study emergent misalignment, highlighting that some harmful fine-tuning examples have outsized impact and suggesting attribution-aware data selection/modulation as a post-training control lever. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37914)

- **Storage Is Not Strategy: State-Conditioned Support Control for LLM Unlearning:** Argues against fixed parameter subsets for localized unlearning, proposing state-conditioned support control that adapts which parameters are updated during optimization for more targeted forgetting. [Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37858)

## Weekly Digest

### Academic / Open Research

#### RL, post-training, and evaluation
- [**DART-ES: Difficulty-Aware Reweighting and Targeted Replay for Fine-Tuning LLMs with Evolution Strategies**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06993) — Improves ES-based full-parameter fine-tuning by reweighting harder examples and replaying informative prompts to boost signal and sample efficiency.
- [**Reward-Driven Learning under Prompt-Level Differential Privacy**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07212) — Explores RL with verifiable rewards when prompts are sensitive, framing prompt-level DP as a practical tool for enterprise post-training on confidential data.
- [**RELACE: retrospective likelihood-based action credit estimation for long-horizon language agents**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07349) — Tackles noisy trajectory-level rewards in multi-turn agents with action-level credit assignment, without requiring a separate critic.
- [**Stabilizing Off-Policy Training for Long-Horizon LLM Agent via Turn-Level Importance Sampling and Clipping-Triggered Normalization**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20718) — Targets instability in off-policy agent RL with turn-level importance sampling and normalization for longer interactions.
- [**What pass@k Cannot Measure: Evaluating Diversity and Capability Retention after Post-Training**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07405) — Argues pass@k can hide reduced diversity and capability loss, pushing for broader post-training evaluation.
- **Harmful SFT Leaves a Continuous Trace in LLM Checkpoint Updates** — Investigates whether harmful supervised fine-tuning can be detected directly from checkpoint deltas rather than behavior alone. *(No link was included in the source notes.)*

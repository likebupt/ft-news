## Google DeepMind

**Google DeepMind and Singapore Government collaborate to build fine-tuned AI model for scam detection**
- Google DeepMind and GovTech Singapore are deploying a fine-tuned, lightweight Gemma-based model to detect conversational scams directly on users’ devices, aiming to protect private chats without routing data to the cloud. [Read more](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)
- The deployment highlights an edge-AI/post-training strategy: compact models optimized for on-device inference can provide real-time scam interception while improving privacy and reducing cloud data egress. [Read more](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)
- This is a notable public-sector use case for fine-tuned small models, showing how customized LLMs can be adapted for high-sensitivity safety applications rather than general-purpose chat alone. [Read more](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)

## Key Takeaways
- Fine-tuned small models are becoming practical for privacy-sensitive mobile and edge deployments, not just cloud-hosted assistants. [Read more](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)
- On-device post-training is emerging as a strong fit for regulated and public-sector AI use cases where data egress is a major constraint. [Read more](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)
- Scam detection is shaping up as a concrete, user-facing safety application for specialized LLMs. [Read more](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)

### Together AI
- Together AI launched a managed post-training offering for open models like Qwen, GLM, and Kimi, supporting both SFT and RL while letting customers define their own training loop. This matters for finetuning because it lowers infrastructure overhead without sacrificing control over recipes, weights, or ownership of the resulting model. [Read more](https://www.together.ai/blog/together-post-training)

### Hugging Face / Microsoft
- Hugging Face highlighted Microsoft’s ThinkingBox approach for turning database-style grading signals into RL rewards for agent post-training. For finetuning, it points to a scalable path for training agents with verifiable, programmatic rewards instead of relying only on costly human preference labels. [Read more](https://huggingface.co/blog/microsoft/thinkingbox-training)

### OpenAI
- OpenAI announced a partnership with Poland’s NASK focused on AI safety, security, and technical research. For finetuning, stronger external safety collaboration can improve the red-teaming, alignment research, and evaluation practices used around post-trained models. [Read more](https://openai.com/index/openai-nask-ai-safety-security-partnership)
- OpenAI described how it works with distribution partners to apply consistent safeguards and usage standards across access channels. This matters for finetuning because customized or downstream model variants increasingly need standardized policy, monitoring, and safety-eval requirements at deployment time. [Read more](https://openai.com/index/broad-access-through-consistent-standards)

### Amazon
- Amazon said it used independent third-party review via AVERI to assess and improve its internal AI safety testing. For finetuning, this reinforces the value of external evaluation as a release gate for aligned post-training and safety-sensitive model updates.
